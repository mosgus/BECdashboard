1Holdings

Build and maintain your portfolio positions. Add, edit, or remove stocks; manage cash allocation.
When to use: Daily basis to track holdings, adjust allocation, import new strategies, or rebalance.
Key features:
  • Add individual stocks (price, shares, thesis)
  • Set cash allocation (dollar amount + target %)
  • Import portfolio CSV templates
  • View portfolio composition & value
  • Monitor HHI concentration index

2Backtest

Test your strategy against historical data. Optimize allocations based on past performance.
When to use: Before committing capital; validating strategic shifts; stress-testing new candidate stocks.
Key features:
  • 8 backtest modes (Equal Weight, Volatility, Sharpe, etc.)
  • Customizable date ranges
  • Walk-forward validation to prevent overfitting
  • Drawdown & volatility analysis
  • Side-by-side strategy comparison

3Outlook

Look forward. Use CAPM to optimize, Monte Carlo for scenarios, and forecasting for price/volatility.
When to use: Forward-looking risk assessment, conviction weighting on disagreements, portfolio resilience checks.
Key features:
  • CAPM Optimizer: Find optimal weights; adjust conviction via "% undervalued"
  • Monte Carlo: 1000s of paths; terminal stats; Brier scoring for calibration
  • Efficient Frontier: Risk-return tradeoff; Max Sharpe & Min Variance portfolios
  • Forecast: Model future price & volatility

4Monitor

Track candidate stocks and ideas. Watch technical signals and fundamental changes.
When to use: Sourcing new opportunities; monitoring macro shifts; technical entry/exit signals.
Key features:
  • Watchlist of external candidates (not yet in portfolio)
  • Technical indicators (SMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands)
  • Price alerts and signal notifications
  • Track ideas from research, news, peer suggestions

5Risk & Perf

Measure and stress-test your portfolio. Understand what's working and what could go wrong.
When to use: Monthly/quarterly reviews; stress testing; attribution analysis; performance post-mortems.
Key features:
  • Performance: CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown vs. benchmark
  • Health: VaR, CVaR, stress test scenarios
  • Attribution: Fama-French factor decomposition
  • Scenarios: What-if analysis (rate shock, vol spike, market crash)
Plus: Research Suite (securities, asset, portfolio, stress) and Watchlists for deeper dives. See BLUEEAGLE_GUIDE.md for full documentation.
