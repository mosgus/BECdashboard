🔬 Research Suite Cheat Sheet

Six Specialized Tools for Deep Dives — What to Look For & When to Act

Overview

Portfolio Snapshot & Positioning

High-level view of your portfolio: holdings breakdown, sector exposure, top positions.

What to Look For
  • Concentration risk (HHI, top 5 positions)
  • Sector imbalance vs. target
  • Correlation clusters
  • Liquidity constraints
When to Act
REVIEW: Quarterly rebalance cycles
RED FLAG: HHI > 0.20 or top position > 20%

Securities Research

Individual Stock Deep Dive

Fundamental analysis, technicals, earnings history, and valuation multiples for any security.

What to Look For
  • Revenue & earnings trends (YoY growth)
  • Valuation vs. peer and sector medians
  • Insider ownership & lock-up schedules
  • Technical support/resistance levels
When to Act
DUE DILIGENCE: Before adding new position
RED FLAG: Missed earnings, downgrade, or technicals break support

Asset Research

Multi-Asset Comparison

Compare returns, volatility, and correlations across your holdings and benchmark.

What to Look For
  • Asset-to-benchmark correlation
  • Risk-adjusted return (Sharpe, Sortino)
  • Drawdown recovery time
  • Relative strength vs. sector/market
When to Act
OPTIMIZE: Quarterly rebalance
YELLOW FLAG: Negative Sharpe & underperforming

Portfolio Research

Entire Fund Performance & Attribution

Aggregate metrics, factor exposure, performance vs. benchmark, and performance sources.

What to Look For
  • CAGR, Sharpe, max drawdown
  • Beta and factor exposures (Fama-French)
  • Contribution to return by asset
  • Performance persistence
When to Act
REVIEW: Monthly with Investment Committee
INVESTIGATE: If underperforming for 2+ months

Stress & Robustness

Scenario Analysis & Tail Risk

Test portfolio under extreme scenarios: market crash, rate shock, volatility spike.

What to Look For
  • Portfolio loss under 2008-style shock
  • Duration sensitivity to rate changes
  • VaR and CVaR at 95% and 99% confidence
  • Systemic risk exposure
When to Act
QUARTERLY: Standard review
URGENT: If 99% CVaR > risk limit

Decision Memo

Structured Investment Thesis

Template-driven memo for documenting conviction, catalysts, downside scenarios, and sizing.

What to Look For
  • Clear investment thesis (bull case)
  • Downside scenario & probability
  • Key catalysts and timeline
  • Recommended position size & entry
When to Act
REQUIRED: Before any new position > 2% of AUM
GOVERNANCE: Investment Committee approval needed

🎯 Research Workflow Summary

Sourcing Phase: Use Securities → Asset Research → Decision Memo

Monitoring Phase: Portfolio Research + Stress & Robustness (Monthly)

Rebalance Phase: Asset Research + Overview (Quarterly)

Post-Mortem Phase: Portfolio Research + Attribution (After significant events)
