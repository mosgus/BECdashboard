🎯 Investment Decision Workflow

From Stock Idea to Position — Structured Approval Process

1 Idea Sourcing

Identify potential stock opportunity from research, news, peer suggestions, or screening.

Action: Add to Watchlist in Monitor tab. Track technicals and fundamentals.

Duration: 1–4 weeks observation period.

↓
2 Preliminary Due Diligence

Run quick checks: fundamentals, valuation, technical setup, competitive position.

Tools: Securities Research tab. Check earnings history, multiples, insider ownership, technicals.

Quick Filter: Does this fit our investment style and sector targets?

If fundamentals are weak or technicals broken: Return to sourcing. Mark for later.
↓
3 Decision Memo Preparation

Formalize investment thesis. Structure bull case, downside scenario, catalysts, position sizing.

Required Sections: Investment Thesis · Bull Case · Bear Case & Probability · Catalysts & Timeline · Entry Point & Target · Recommended Size

Use Decision Memo tab in Research Suite to draft and document.

Position Size Rule:
• < 1% of AUM: Team lead approval
• 1–2% of AUM: One committee member sign-off
• > 2% of AUM: Full Investment Committee approval (required)
↓
4 Investment Committee Review

Present memo to committee. Q&A on thesis, risks, competitive advantages, macro alignment.

Committee Questions: What if the bull case doesn't play out? What's the exit plan? How does this fit our mandate?

Timeline: Typically 1–2 weeks for feedback loop.

🟡 CONDITIONAL GO: Memo approved with modifications. Revise and resubmit, or proceed with smaller size.
🟢 GO: Memo approved as-is. Proceed to position entry.
🔴 NO-GO: Memo rejected. Risk/return doesn't justify entry. Archive for future reconsideration.
↓
5 Position Entry

Execute trade at approved entry point and size. Document in Holdings tab.

Action: Add position to Holdings. Set thesis comment. Link to Decision Memo.
Target Setup: Enter target price from memo. Set stop-loss if applicable.

Size Check: Verify HHI < 0.20 post-entry. Adjust other positions if needed.

↓
6 Monitoring & Rebalancing

Track position performance. Watch for catalyst triggers and fundamental changes.

Monthly Check: Portfolio Research tab. Is conviction still valid? Any negative catalyst?
Quarterly Rebalance: Use Asset Research + Backtest tabs. Optimize allocation via CAPM.
🟡 THESIS AT RISK: Catalyst delayed, or new headwind. Reduce size or add to observation list.
🟢 ON TRACK: Catalyst playing out as expected. Hold or add if target not yet met.
🔴 THESIS BROKEN: Original bull case no longer valid. Exit position. Document post-mortem in Decision Memo.
↓
7 Exit & Post-Mortem

Liquidate position at target or stop-loss. Document what went right and what went wrong.

Action: Remove from Holdings. Archive Decision Memo with exit notes.
Post-Mortem: Compare target vs. actual return. Analyze catalyst timing. Was thesis timing off or thesis wrong?

Learning: Feed insights back into future thesis-building. Store in Research archive for institutional memory.

🏛️ Governance & Approvals

Position Size Thresholds:

  • < 1%: Team lead (portfolio manager) approval sufficient
  • 1–2%: One Investment Committee member sign-off
  • > 2%: Full Investment Committee approval required
  • > 5%: Escalate to Fund Advisor (Dalio or designated)

Decision Memo Requirements:

  • Clear investment thesis (1–2 sentences)
  • Bull case with catalysts and timeline
  • Bear case with downside probability estimate
  • Valuation: entry price, target price, stop-loss
  • Position size and HHI impact check
  • Risk disclosure (liquidity, geopolitical, sector headwinds)

📋 Workflow Summary

Fast Track (Minor Position < 1%): Sourcing → Quick Due Diligence → Simple Memo → Lead Approval → Entry → Monitor

Standard Track (1–2% Position): Sourcing → DD → Full Memo → Committee Review → Entry → Monitor → Rebalance

Major Position (> 2%): Sourcing → Deep DD → Formal Memo → Full Committee + Advisor Approval → Entry → Active Monitoring → Quarterly Rebalance + Monthly Check-ins
